YouTube

Desktop Browser

Step 1 of 2
Click 'Share' button

Under the video player, click the Share button, then click Copy next to the link.

YouTube Mobile

iOS & Android App

Step 2 of 2
Tap 'Copy Link'

Tap the Share arrow below the video title, then tap Copy Link from the popup menu.

YouTube Music

Web & Mobile

Audio Track
Options (⋮) → Share

Click or tap the three dots (⋮) next to any song or album title, select Share, then Copy link.

Other Websites

Vimeo, Soundcloud, etc.

Universal
Copy Address Bar URL

Simply highlight the URL in your browser's address bar at the top, right-click, and select Copy.

Pro tip

Paste one link per line in the app's text box. You can paste dozens at once — it will download them one after the other automatically.
